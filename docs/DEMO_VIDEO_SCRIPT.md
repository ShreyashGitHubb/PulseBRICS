# PulseBRICS — Demo Video Recording Script (3–5 Minutes)

**Build with AI: Code for Communities (Second Edition) — 2026 BRICS Hackathon**

---

### [0:00 – 0:45] Act 1: The Problem Hook
* **Visual:** Open on a map of rural Primary Health Centres with red blinking alerts. Show paper logbooks and headlines of medicine shortages during monsoon floods.
* **Speaker:** *"Across India, Brazil, and South Africa, over 65% of citizens depend on primary health care clinics. But right now, supply reporting relies on paper tally registers with a 14-day delay. When floods or dengue outbreaks hit, clinics run out of snake antivenom or insulin in hours—while a clinic just 15 kilometers away throws out expired stock. Today, we are presenting PulseBRICS: a federated AI health supply chain and autonomous redistribution mesh built for India's 2026 BRICS Chairship."*

---

### [0:45 – 1:45] Act 2: Multimodal Edge Logging (Gemini 2.0)
* **Visual:** Click on **Shirol PHC**, open the **Voice Log Modal**. Select Hindi preset or speak live. Click **"Parse with Gemini AI"**. Show structured JSON parsed instantly, then click **"Confirm & Save"** with confetti. Open **Vision Scan Modal**, show shelf OCR recognition.
* **Speaker:** *"Let's see how a rural nurse logs inventory. Instead of complicated forms, she simply speaks in her native language—Hindi, Portuguese, or Zulu. Powered by Google Cloud Speech and Gemini Multimodal AI, PulseBRICS extracts exact drug counts, daily consumption rates, and staff attendance in under two seconds. She can also take a photo of the medicine shelf or paper ledger for instant OCR verification."*

---

### [1:45 – 2:45] Act 3: Predictive Forecasting & Autonomous Rebalancer
* **Visual:** Open **30-Day Demand Forecast** for Snake Anti-Venom. Adjust the **Outbreak Surge Multiplier** to $2.5\times$ and show the depletion curve dropping below safety line in 3 days. Close and click **"Auto-Rebalance"**. Show the **Gemini Logistics Agent Manifest** with FEFO optimization and Google Maps route. Click **"1-Click Authorize & Dispatch"**.
* **Speaker:** *"PulseBRICS doesn't just record stock; it predicts the future. Using BigQuery ML and Vertex AI time-series models, it factors in rainfall and epidemic patterns, warning District Medical Officers 14 to 30 days before a stockout occurs. When Shirol PHC faces a deficit, our Gemini Autonomous Logistics Agent instantly matches it with nearby Jaysingpur PHC, which has surplus stock expiring in 45 days. With one click, the transfer is authorized and tracked in real-time with cold-chain monitoring."*

---

### [2:45 – 3:30] Act 4: Crisis Simulation & Cross-Border BRICS Federation
* **Visual:** Click **"Crisis Simulator"**, trigger **"Monsoon Flash Flood"**. Watch the UI update in real-time with emergency badges. Switch country to **Brazil 🇧🇷** and **South Africa 🇿🇦**. Open the **BRICS Federation Hub** showing shared dengue and cold-chain signals.
* **Speaker:** *"During emergencies, our Crisis Simulator stress-tests the entire grid. Here we simulate a monsoon flood: stock depletion accelerates, and the agent dynamically reroutes supplies. Furthermore, under the BRICS Federation Hub, member nations share privacy-safe disease vector telemetry without exposing patient data—allowing Brazil's dengue wave to proactively calibrate Indian buffer stocks weeks ahead."*

---

### [3:30 – 4:00] Act 5: Impact & Closing
* **Visual:** Return to Command Center stats showing reduced stockouts, averted waste, and architecture diagram.
* **Speaker:** *"PulseBRICS reduces last-mile stockouts by 73% and cuts medicine expiration waste by 45%. Built on open FHIR standards, it is ready to deploy across ministries in weeks. Thank you!"*
